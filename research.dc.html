Research statement

Transformations

I study organizations, fields, and industries as they undergo digital transformation and pursue sustainability goals. From a theoretical standpoint, I study organizational and institutional factors behind these transformations, and the impacts they have. This agenda builds directly on my prior master's degrees in sustainability management and information technology management, and on years of research experience before and during my PhD.

Dissertation overview

Corporate commitments to stakeholders

My dissertation examines three types of transformation that follow organizational commitments to three groups of stakeholders: internal stakeholders, community stakeholders, and the broader society.

Internal stakeholders

Agent or Steward: Unconstrained Power and Managerial Organizational Identification

Amirsalar Jafari Gorizi, Mike W. Peng PhD

Examines how constraints on shareholder power shape managers' identification with their organization. Contributes to the agency and stewardship literatures by reconciling their competing predictions. Earlier version nominated for the SMS Best Student Paper Award. Targeted for the Academy of Management Journal.

Community stakeholders

Contractual Commitments and Stakeholder Conflicts

Amirsalar Jafari Gorizi, Mike W. Peng PhD

Studies contractual commitments between multinational natural resource firms and local community stakeholders. Contributes to the literature on contract design and community mobilization. Earlier version won the EAOM Best Student Paper Award. Targeted for the Journal of International Business Studies.

Broader society

Corporate Sustainability Pledges

Amirsalar Jafari Gorizi, Mike W. Peng PhD, Sahar Moslemian

Studies why firms join the United Nations Global Compact and how financial markets respond. Contributes to research on corporate sustainability and market perceptions of pledges. AOM Proceedings 2026. Targeted for Management Science.

Microlinguistic Persuasion Mechanisms in Corporate Communication

Amirsalar Jafari Gorizi

Examines microlinguistic mechanisms of persuasion in corporate political content. Solo-authored. Recently submitted.

How digital Transformation influence Agenda-setting Function of Newspapers

Amirsalar Jafari Gorizi, Weimin Liang

Studies how digital transformation altered agenda-setting at a major U.S. newspaper, using an affordance-based institutional logics lens. Contributes to research on digital transformation of professional practices. Short version published in ICIS Proceedings 2025. Current stage: Preparing for submission.

An Empirical Investigation of LLMs’ Influence on Digital Publication Platforms

Amirsalar Jafari Gorizi, Anna Fung PhD

Studies digital publication platforms to examine how large language model affordances intersect with professional routines. Contributes to research on digital transformation of professional practice. Current stage: Preparing for submission. Will Present at DSI 2026.

Geopolitical Shocks and Environmental Degradation

Amirsalar Jafari Gorizi

Integrates real options theory with institutional polycentricism to examine how geopolitical pressure shapes environmental outcomes in the natural resource industry. Contributes to research on multinational firms and corporate sustainability. Current stage: Data analyses. Will present at AIB NE 2026.

Drug Liberalization and Empowerment of Communities through Entrepreneurship

Amirsalar Jafari Gorizi, Alessandro Piazza PhD

Asks whether cannabis legalization spills over into broader entrepreneurship. Contributes to research on regulation and local economic activity. Presented at the Lone Star Conference, Aug 2026. Current stage: Analyses done, In writing phase.
