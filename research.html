Transformations

I study organizations, fields, and industries as they go through digital transformation and pursue sustainability goals.

From a theoretical standpoint, I look at the organizational and institutional factors behind these transformations, and the impacts they have. This agenda builds on my master's degrees in sustainability management and information technology management, and on years of research experience before and during my PhD.

Dissertation: corporate commitments to stakeholders

Three papers, each following a different kind of transformation that begins when a firm commits to a group of stakeholders.

Internal stakeholders

Agent or Steward: Unconstrained Power and Managerial Organizational Identification

with Mike W. Peng

How constraints on shareholder power shape managers' identification with their organization. Reconciles the competing predictions of agency and stewardship theory.

Nominated for the SMS Best Student Paper Award. Presenting at the Corporate Strategy and Innovation Conference, Wharton, Dec 2026. Targeted for the Academy of Management Journal.

Community stakeholders

Contractual Commitments and Stakeholder Conflicts

with Mike W. Peng

Contractual commitments between multinational natural resource firms and local communities. Contributes to the literature on contract design and community mobilization.

Won the EAOM Best Student Paper Award. Targeted for the Journal of International Business Studies.

Broader society

Corporate Sustainability Pledges

with Mike W. Peng and Sahar Moslemian

Why firms join the United Nations Global Compact, and how financial markets respond. Contributes to research on corporate sustainability and market perceptions of pledges.

AOM Proceedings 2026. Targeted for Management Science.

Working papers

Microlinguistic Persuasion Mechanisms in Corporate Communication

Solo-authored

Microlinguistic mechanisms of persuasion in corporate political content. Recently submitted.

Rhetorical Legitimacy Seeking of Onshore Investment Commitments Under Universal Tariffs

with Mike W. Peng

Uses the metaphor of funambulism to argue that firms use different rhetoric when talking to two audiences about onshore investment commitments. Preparing for submission.

How Digital Transformation Influences the Agenda-Setting Function of Newspapers

with Weimin Liang

How digital transformation altered agenda-setting at a major U.S. newspaper, through an affordance-based institutional logics lens. Short version in ICIS Proceedings 2025. Preparing for submission.

An Empirical Investigation of LLMs' Influence on Digital Publication Platforms

How large language model affordances intersect with professional routines on digital publication platforms. Presenting at DSI 2026. Preparing for submission.

Geopolitical Shocks and Environmental Degradation

Solo-authored

Integrates real options theory with institutional polycentricism to examine how geopolitical pressure shapes environmental outcomes in the natural resource industry. Presented at AIB NE 2026. Data analysis.

Drug Liberalization and Empowerment of Communities Through Entrepreneurship

Does cannabis legalization spill over into broader entrepreneurship? Presented at the Lone Star Conference, Aug 2026. Data analysis and writing.
